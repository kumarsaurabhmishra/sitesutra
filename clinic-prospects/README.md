## Outreach draft for Ravi Dental Care

> Namaste, main Saurabh, SiteSutra se, Varanasi mein website design karta hoon. Ravi Dental Care ka Google Maps profile dekha—patients ko aapka address aur phone milta hai, lekin website link listed nahi hai. Maine aapke liye ek one-page website concept banaya hai jo clinic location, direct call aur appointment enquiry ko clearly dikhata hai: [preview link]. Yeh sirf review ke liye independent concept hai. Agar aapko direction useful lage, main aapke confirmed clinic details, photos aur branding ke saath final version bana sakta hoon. Kya main aapko 10-minute walkthrough dikha sakta hoon?

Suggested first offer if they ask for terms: ₹11,000 for one responsive page, two content revisions, call and directions buttons, and launch support. The clinic owns its domain and hosting account and pays those provider charges directly. Agree on any ongoing updates and their price separately. Confirm final scope and price with the user before sending an actual quote.

Follow-up after 3–4 days, once only: “Namaste, Ravi Dental Care ke website concept ka link share kiya tha. Agar useful ho, main 10 minutes mein layout aur required clinic details dikha sakta hoon. Koi interest na ho to bhi bilkul theek.”

Before handover, ask the clinic to confirm the practice name, treatment list, credentials, consultation process, hours, fees, official photos, and preferred booking method. Remove the preview label only after the clinic accepts the project and approves the final content.
