# SiteSutra SEO review — 31 October 2026

## What the current evidence says

Search Console has 54 impressions and no clicks from 16–28 October; impressions begin on 18 October. The weighted average position in the export is 65.4. The largest page row is `/services/seo-marketing/` with 25 impressions at position 89.84. The leading query, `technical seo audit services india`, has 10 impressions at position 90.2. These results are too low on the page to provide a meaningful click opportunity, so the present evidence points to limited ranking and authority rather than a demonstrated title or description failure.

Google reports 22 indexed pages and seven not indexed. One is an intentionally excluded concept demo and six are “Discovered – currently not indexed” with validation in progress. The sitemap is successful, public pages have indexable directives and self-referencing canonicals, and Search Console reports no manual action. Core Web Vitals has no field report because Chrome has not collected enough usage data. Search Console sees four external links, all from GitHub. This is a new property with eleven days containing impressions and very little independent web authority.

The dated aggregate data is in `measurement/gsc-observation-2026-10-31.json`. Raw Search Console exports remain private and outside this repository.

## Changes made in this review

1. Added global descriptive links to the two priority service pages so visitors and crawlers do not have to rely on the services overview or old article links.
2. Added a direct technical SEO audit worksheet to the service page. It shows the expected evidence, owner and verification fields instead of making a generic service claim.
3. Added the verified phone and email as an Organization sales contact in structured data. The schema remains Organization rather than LocalBusiness because no precise eligible address or service-area profile has been verified.
4. Added article `lastmod` dates to the sitemap from the existing publication records. Static pages omit `lastmod` rather than emitting a build date that does not reflect meaningful content change.
5. Kept the current titles, H1s and four-page keyword map stable. The current sample does not justify another title test, extra city pages or separate pages for consultant/company/agency wording.

## Owner actions that code cannot complete

1. Create and verify an eligible Google Business Profile using the real working arrangement. Complete services, hours, phone, website and business description. Request reviews only from real customers and do not gate or fabricate them.
2. Supply genuine professional-profile URLs for the founder and business. Use the same approved name, phone, domain and service information across those profiles.
3. Replace concept-only proof with permissioned client work as it becomes available. Record the brief, actual role, deliverables and approved outcome without inventing performance.
4. Earn relevant references through real relationships: agency partner pages, professional associations, local business listings with editorial standards, supplier/technology profiles and useful resources that another site chooses to cite. Avoid purchased link packages and mass directory submissions.
5. Configure a real enquiry endpoint or maintain a private lead register so received and qualified enquiries can be evaluated separately from clicks.

## Measurement plan

Review indexing weekly for errors, not to repeatedly request the same URLs. Compare equal completed 28-day Search Console windows once the property has enough data. Track the four canonical service groups, India and device segments, and the pages that actually receive impressions. Review snippets only when a page has a meaningful number of impressions at positions where clicks are plausible. Review genuine enquiries in a private record. A ranking or click change cannot be promised or attributed from this small baseline.
