# SiteSutra SEO and accessibility audit — 21 November 2026

## Executive finding

SiteSutra has a sound technical search foundation. The reviewed public pages return successful responses, expose their primary content without JavaScript, use self-referencing canonicals, contain one primary heading, and are linked through a valid sitemap. No critical crawl or indexing blocker was found.

The largest constraint is not a missing meta tag. SiteSutra is a young domain with limited independent references and limited verified commercial evidence. Technical improvements can make crawling, accessibility and performance more reliable, but they cannot guarantee rankings or enquiries.

## Scope and evidence

- Reviewed the live homepage, principal service pages, agency page, About page and Contact page.
- Validated `robots.txt`, the XML sitemap and all 57 sitemap URLs.
- Checked rendered primary content with JavaScript disabled.
- Checked representative crawler user agents and real 404 behaviour.
- Ran a mobile lab audit across 20 representative routes.
- Ran the repository's link, metadata, schema, sitemap, target-map and accessibility-oriented verification suite.

The public PageSpeed Insights API quota was unavailable during the audit. Mobile performance observations below are local laboratory measurements, not field data from real visitors.

## Findings

### Crawl and indexability

- `robots.txt` allows public pages and declares the sitemap.
- The sitemap contains 57 unique, valid public URLs.
- Public pages use current-domain canonicals and index/follow directives.
- Private paths remain disallowed.
- Missing routes return a genuine 404 response.
- Primary content remains readable without animation or client-side rendering.

### Metadata and page targeting

- Titles, descriptions and H1s are unique across the indexable set.
- Dedicated pages already own the principal website-development, Varanasi marketing, technical SEO and agency-partnership topics.
- Service pages explain audience, scope and next steps without relying on repeated keyword variants.
- Structured data identifies SiteSutra, Kumar Saurabh Mishra, services, articles and page relationships without ratings or invented credentials.

### Accessibility and mobile experience

The mobile lab sample scored well overall, but found specific homepage and agency-page defects:

1. The animated benefit statement used an ARIA label on an unsupported element and hid its visible words from assistive technology.
2. The statement's unrevealed text had insufficient contrast.
3. The mobile menu's programmatic name did not contain its visible label.
4. Example-card links used explicit labels that did not match their visible text.
5. One concept image did not declare intrinsic dimensions.

These defects are corrected in this change. The menu now exposes the same visible and accessible label, the animated statement remains in the accessibility tree, its muted state exceeds the relevant contrast threshold, example links use their visible content as their name, and the concept image reserves its correct aspect ratio.

### Performance

Representative mobile lab scores ranged from 0.82 to 0.96 for performance, with zero total blocking time on the reviewed homepage and agency page. The main avoidable delay was an external Google Fonts stylesheet and font chain.

Manrope is now served from the SiteSutra deployment and the primary Latin font is preloaded. This removes the external stylesheet dependency, reduces a render-blocking chain and avoids sending font requests to Google. This is a performance and privacy improvement, not a ranking promise.

### Authority and trust

The remaining high-impact gap sits outside the codebase:

- Build a complete and verified Google Business Profile.
- Keep name, phone, website and location details consistent on legitimate profiles and directories.
- Earn relevant references through real partnerships, useful original resources and permitted project features.
- Add real case studies and reviews only when they can be verified and published with permission.
- Replace demonstration-heavy proof with commissioned work as it becomes available.

Buying links, mass directory submissions, fabricated reviews and copied competitor content are excluded because they create quality and policy risk.

## Measurement plan

Track three separate outcomes:

1. **Search visibility:** indexed URLs, impressions, clicks, query/page pairs and average position in Search Console.
2. **External citations:** legitimate referring domains and mentions, reviewed separately from search traffic.
3. **Qualified enquiries:** booked calls, sent email enquiries and accepted project conversations. The current form only prepares an email draft, so it must not be counted as a delivered enquiry.

Review Search Console after the deployment has been live long enough to appear in complete reporting data. Compare query/page pairs rather than treating sitewide impressions alone as success.
